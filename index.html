Problem Statement

Everything at Neth Opticians runs on paper today.

Patient records, prescriptions and orders are written in books and files. Finding an old prescription means searching by hand through past registers. Order and billing status is tracked verbally — mistakes are easy. Stock levels of frames and lenses are only known by memory. No reliable way to follow up patients or report on monthly performance.

Lost History

Old records are hard or impossible to retrieve

Slow Service

Repeat visits take longer than they should

Stock Blind Spots

Re-ordering happens too late

No Insight

Sales and follow-ups are never measured

Solution Overview

Nethcare: one shared record, four connected modules.

A single web-based system where every patient, examination, order and payment lives in one database and each role sees exactly what they need.

The Four Modules

M1 Patient Registration & History · Gayathri

Goal — make every patient identifiable in seconds and keep their full history in one file.

  • Register a new patient with contact, age and medical notes
  • Search and open any existing patient record instantly
  • Patient portal: view own profile, prescriptions and order status
  • Admin console: create users, assign roles, deactivate accounts
  • Every visit appended to a chronological history timeline
Key entities: Patient, User, Role, Visit
Primary actors: Patient, Optician, Admin
Depends on: Nothing — foundation module
RuleDetail
Unique patientNIC (or phone for minors) identifies a patient; duplicates blocked at save
MinorsGuardian name and contact mandatory for patients under 18
History is append-onlyPast visits can never be deleted, only corrected with a new entry
Portal accessPatients see their own record only, read-only, via phone-number login code
DeactivationAdmin deactivates staff accounts; records created by them remain intact

M2 Examination, Prescription & Referral · Akshai

Goal — capture what the optician sees, turn it into a prescription, and hand off cleanly when a surgeon is needed.

  • Structured eye-examination form (VA, SPH, CYL, AXIS, ADD, IPD)
  • Prescription generated from the examination and stored forever
  • Full prescription history with change comparison over time
  • Referral to an eye surgeon with reason and attached history
  • Surgeon feedback returned into the patient file
Key entities: Examination, Prescription, Referral, Surgeon
Primary actors: Optician, Eye Surgeon, Patient
Depends on: Module 1 — patient must exist first
RuleDetail
Prescription validityValid for 12 months; older ones flagged before ordering
ImmutableOnce issued, cannot be edited — only superseded by a new examination
Referral triggerOptician may refer at any point; referral always carries last 4 prescriptions
Contact lensesRecord base curve and diameter in addition to standard fields
Surgeon accessSurgeons see referred patients only, for 90 days after referral

M3 Order, Billing & Stock · Shagash

Goal — turn a prescription into a priced order, a correct bill, and an accurate stock position.

  • Create an order from a prescription: frame, lens type, coatings
  • Automatic pricing, discount and advance-payment handling
  • Printable bill and receipt with outstanding balance
  • Order status tracking: placed → lab → ready → collected
  • Stock deducted on issue, with low-stock alerts for frames and lenses
Key entities: Order, OrderItem, Bill, Payment, StockItem
Primary actors: Staff, Admin, Patient
Depends on: Module 2 — prescription drives lens spec
RuleDetail
Price sourceAdmin maintains a price list; order uses price at time of order
Advance paymentMinimum 40% advance before order is sent to the lab
Stock deductionReserved at order creation, deducted when order is collected
Low stockItem below reorder level appears on admin dashboard automatically
CancellationsCancelled order releases reserved stock and records a credit note

M4 Follow-up, Reporting & Audit · Edwien

Goal — bring patients back on time, show the owner how the shop is performing, and keep an unbreakable record of who did what.

  • Weekly follow-up list of patients due for a re-check
  • SMS / email notifications: order ready, appointment, reminder
  • Management reports: monthly sales, patients attended, order status
  • Stock summary and low-stock report for re-ordering
  • Immutable audit log of every create, update and delete
Key entities: FollowUp, Notification, Report, AuditLog
Primary actors: Admin, Notification service, Audit
Depends on: Data from Modules 1, 2 and 3
RuleDetail
Audit coverageEvery create, update, delete stores user, action, entity, old value, timestamp
ImmutableAudit rows cannot be edited or deleted by any role, including admin
Follow-up window12 months after last examination; contact-lens users at 6 months
Notification channelsSMS first, email as fallback; patients may opt out at any time
Report accessFinancial reports visible to owner and admin only

Design Principles

3NF relational schema, surrogate primary keys, FK enforced at DB level, soft-delete only.

Integrity

A bill cannot exist without an order; an order cannot exist without a valid prescription; a prescription cannot exist without an examination.

Security

Role-based access at query level, encrypted patient identifiers, daily backups, append-only audit table.

Open Questions for the Client

Historical Records: Should we bulk-enter past prescriptions, or start from go-live?

Advance Payment: Is 40% advance the correct minimum for all orders?
Notifications: SMS, WhatsApp or both — and who pays the message cost?
Referral Network: Which eye surgeons should be pre-loaded into the system?
Language: Do patient-facing screens and bills need Sinhala as well as English?
Access: Should the owner be able to view the system from home?
