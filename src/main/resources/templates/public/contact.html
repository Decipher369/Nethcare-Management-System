A few helpful details.

What should I bring for an eye test?+

Bring your current glasses and any previous prescription you have. Let us know about any changes you’ve noticed in your vision.

Can I try frames on in the shop?+

Yes. Visit us to try different shapes and find a comfortable fit. We can help you choose frames to suit your prescription and preferences.

Do you offer repairs and adjustments?+

We help with frame repairs, hinges and temples. Adjustments are free for glasses bought at Neth Opticians. Bring your pair in so we can take a look.

Can I buy online?+

The online collection helps you explore our range. Fitting, orders and payment are handled in the shop, where we can make sure everything is right for you.
