Neth Opticians / Kolonnawa

Eye care with
a human touch.

Great eyewear starts with understanding the person wearing it. At Neth Opticians, we bring eye examinations, prescription lenses and frame fitting together in one place.

Led by Ms. Udeni Gurusinghe, our shop makes room for the details: how your glasses sit, what your eyes need, and what feels right for you.

We manage the eyecare and optical service at Siri Suwasetha Medical Centre, affiliated to YIMBA - Kolonnawa. The centre has served the community since 2001.
